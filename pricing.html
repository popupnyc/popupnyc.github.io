💡

Your event budget is separate from our fee

Every proposal includes a line-by-line event budget covering venue, vendors, permits, staffing, and build costs. This budget is funded by you into a dedicated account before we pay any vendor. We never front costs ourselves and we never spend without your approval. Any unused budget is returned to you after the event with a full reconciliation statement.

Clarity on scope

What our fee
does not cover

These costs are real and normal parts of any NYC event. They sit in your event budget, not our fee, and you approve every one before we commit to it.

🏢

Venue rental

The cost of the space itself. This varies widely depending on neighborhood, size, and duration. We negotiate on your behalf and present options across a range of price points.

🚚

Vendor costs

Furniture rental, fabrication, florals, A/V, signage, catering, and any other physical elements of the event. All quoted and approved before we book anything.

👥

On-site staffing

Brand ambassadors, check-in hosts, sales associates. We source, brief, and manage them. Their hourly rates come from your event budget, not our fee.

📋

Permits and filing fees

NYC permit costs vary by event type and location. We handle the filing. The fees themselves are paid from your event budget and shown in your proposal upfront.

📸

Photography and video

Content crew day rates are part of your event budget. We coordinate everything. Included in Full Production package coordination, but the crew cost is yours.

🛡️

Event insurance

We carry general liability insurance on all events. Additional coverage specific to your event or venue requirements is a separate cost and flagged in your proposal.

Payment structure

How and when
you pay

Designed so you're never out of pocket and we're never chasing reimbursement. Simple, fair, and fully transparent.

1
50% deposit at contract signing

This kicks off the project and secures your place in our schedule. It covers venue scouting, moodboarding, planning calls, and all work done before vendors are engaged. Due via invoice when you sign.

2
Event budget funded before vendor payments

Before we pay a single vendor, you fund the event budget into a dedicated account. We pay from there with your sign-off on every commitment. You receive a statement of every payment made.

3
Remaining 50% due one week before the event

The final balance of our production fee is due seven days before your event. By this point, all vendors are confirmed and the run-of-show is locked. Any unused event budget is returned to you within five business days of wrap.

Questions

Pricing questions,
answered directly

Can I get a quote before committing to a package?
+
Yes, always. Fill out our inquiry form and we'll send a full written proposal within 48 hours. This includes our recommended package, a line-by-line event budget estimate, and our production fee. No commitment or deposit required until you decide to move forward.
What if my event is smaller or simpler than the Essentials package?
+
Reach out and tell us what you have in mind. Smaller activations are possible and we'll tell you honestly whether the scope warrants our involvement. We'd rather give you a realistic answer upfront than take on a project that isn't a good fit for either of us.
Can I add services to a package after we've started?
+
Yes. If your scope expands during planning, we'll issue a change order with the additional cost clearly stated. Nothing gets added to your invoice without your written approval first.
Is the deposit refundable if I cancel?
+
The 50% deposit covers real work already done and is non-refundable once planning has begun. Any event budget that hasn't been committed to vendors is returned in full. If you're postponing rather than cancelling, reach out and we'll work with you on rescheduling.
Do you charge more for events outside Manhattan?
+
Not as a rule. Our production fee is based on scope and complexity, not location within NYC. Williamsburg, Long Island City, and other boroughs are no problem. If a project required significant travel outside New York City, we'd discuss that upfront in the proposal.
What does Enterprise or large-scale production look like?
+
Enterprise engagements are fully custom. We've managed six-figure event budgets for brand campaigns, multi-day activations, and agency partnerships. At this level you get a dedicated senior producer, white-glove vendor sourcing, full press and influencer amplification support, and priority access throughout the year. If you're planning something significant, reach out and we'll schedule a strategy call to scope it properly.
What's included in the Premium package specifically?
+
Premium is built for brands doing high-impact activations — multi-day events, custom fabrication, a full press and influencer program, and campaign-level content strategy. You get everything in Full Production plus a dedicated on-site production team, weekly strategy calls throughout the project, and an executive-level post-event debrief. It's the right choice when the activation is a major brand moment, not just an event.
