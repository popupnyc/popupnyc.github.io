Client type 01

Fashion & Beauty Brands

In fashion and beauty, how you show up is the product. We produce activations that match the aesthetic quality of your brand — the right neighborhood, the right space, the right energy. Whether it's an intimate press preview in Nolita or a multi-day launch in Soho, we set the stage so the event looks as good in person as it does on camera.

🗺️

Neighborhood expertise

Nolita for editorial cool. Soho for foot traffic and visibility. Williamsburg for a younger crowd. We recommend the right location for your brand, not just what's available.

📸

Built for content

Every element of the space is considered from a photography perspective. We design environments that look incredible on camera because that's where most of the reach really comes from.

🎟️

Press and influencer ready

We coordinate arrivals, gifting setup, and check-in flow so press and creator guests have a seamless experience from the moment they walk in.

What a great NYC fashion pop-up actually takes

The right neighborhood for your specific customer. A space that photographs from every angle, not just the hero wall. Staff who speak your brand naturally. A content plan built in from day one, not bolted on at the end. And a clear shareable moment designed into the space. We think about all of this so you don't have to.

Press preview Product launch Sample sale Influencer event Fashion week Showroom pop-up Seasonal campaign

Client type 02

DTC & E-Commerce Brands

You've built your brand online. A New York pop-up is the moment you meet your customers in real life, and for many DTC brands it's a turning point. Done right, it builds loyalty no email sequence can replicate. We translate your online identity into a physical space that feels authentic, not like a trade show booth.

🔄

Online to offline, done right

Your digital identity, color palette, and aesthetic references translated into a real environment. Not a generic space with your logo on it. Your brand, in three dimensions.

📦

Product experience first

DTC pop-ups work best when customers can touch, try, or experience the product in a way they can't online. We design the event flow around those discovery moments.

📊

Results that outlast the event

Email capture, customer data, and conversion tracking built into the experience from the start. Your pop-up generates value long after the doors close.

Common event types
Product launch Retail pop-up Brand experience Sample sale Community event Collab drop Limited drop

Why NYC is the right market for your first physical moment

New York has the highest concentration of your target customer, media, and content creators of any US city. A well-executed NYC pop-up generates press and brand awareness that reaches far beyond the city. We've seen brands walk away from a single three-day activation with months of content and national press coverage.

Client type 03

International Brands Entering the US

Your first New York activation is a statement. It tells press, buyers, and customers that you're playing at the highest level. We become your full on-the-ground team for the project, handling everything locally so you can be fully present in the room without being in the room.

🏙️

Your NYC team, for the project

We handle all vendor relationships, permit filings, contracts, and day-of management locally. One trusted contact who owns it end to end and keeps you updated at every stage.

🔄

Fully remote friendly

All approvals happen asynchronously. Calls are scheduled around your time zone. We've worked with clients across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. The process is built for it.

💱

International payments, no friction

We invoice in USD and accept wire transfers via Wise with no surprise conversion fees. A full budget reconciliation statement is delivered after every event.

Why New York is where the US story gets written

National media, wholesale buyers, cultural tastemakers, and the global fashion and beauty press cycle all run through this city. A well-executed NYC activation generates reach that travels to every other US market. It's the most efficient place to make your American introduction.

Client type 04

Corporate & B2B Clients

Corporate events in New York require a different kind of precision. Clients, investors, and partners are in the room, and every detail reflects on your company. We produce events where everything runs on time, everything looks right, and your team can focus entirely on the people they're there to impress.

🏛️

The right venue for your audience

Private dining rooms, hotel event spaces, branded lofts, rooftop terraces. We source venues that fit your industry and the impression you want to make.

🎯

Every detail managed

Guest arrival, A/V, catering timing, branded materials. We manage everything with the same rigor we bring to consumer events, because your guests notice it all.

🤝

Your team stays focused

The worst corporate event is one where your team is chasing caterers instead of clients. We handle all of it so your people can focus on the relationships that matter.

Client dinner Product showcase Conference activation Team event Investor event Annual client evening Press launch

What we handle so you don't have to

Guest list and RSVP management. Run-of-show scripted to the minute. A/V tested before guests arrive. Catering timed to the program. Branded materials produced and proofed. An on-site manager present for the full duration. You show up and lead the room. We handle everything else.
