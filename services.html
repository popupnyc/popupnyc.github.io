Common questions

Everything you might
want to know

How far in advance do I need to book?
+
For most activations, we recommend a minimum of 4–6 weeks of lead time. This gives us enough runway to source and secure the right venue, file any necessary permits, and get all vendors confirmed without rushing. That said, we've produced events in tighter windows — if your timeline is urgent, reach out and tell us what you're working with. We'll be honest about what's realistic.
What is the difference between your fee and the event budget?
+
Our production fee is what you pay us for our time, expertise, and management. The event budget is the money that pays for the actual event — venue rental, vendors, staffing, permits, build costs, catering, and so on. These are always kept separate and clearly presented in your proposal. We never mix them up or hide costs. You'll always see exactly where every dollar is going before you approve anything.
Do I need to be in New York for the event?
+
Not at all — that's the whole point. We're designed to work with clients who are remote. You'll receive regular updates, photos, and real-time communication throughout build and event days. Many of our clients have never set foot in the venue we've used for them. If you want to attend, of course you're welcome. But it's never required for the event to go smoothly.
How does payment work?
+
We require a 50% deposit of our production fee at contract signing, and the remaining 50% is due 7 days before the event. Your event budget is held in a separate account and funded by you before we pay any vendors — we never front costs from our own pocket, and we never ask you to reimburse us after the fact. For international clients, we accept wire transfers via Wise and invoice in USD.
Can you work with brands that have never done a pop-up before?
+
Yes — and honestly, first-timers are some of our favorite clients to work with. You don't need to come in with a fully formed brief or a detailed vision. We'll guide you through it: what questions to ask yourself, what budget ranges are realistic for what you want, which neighborhoods make sense for your brand, and what a reasonable timeline looks like. We're a resource, not just an executor.
What neighborhoods in NYC do you work in?
+
All of them — but we have deep relationships in the neighborhoods that most brand activations happen in: Soho, Nolita, the Lower East Side, Williamsburg, Greenpoint, the Meatpacking District, Chelsea, and Midtown. Each neighborhood has its own personality, foot traffic patterns, and permit considerations. Part of our value is knowing which one is actually right for your brand and your goals.
Do you handle pop-up permits in NYC?
+
Yes. NYC permitting is complicated and varies depending on your event type, location, and what you're doing — sidewalk use, sound amplification, food service, and temporary signage all have different requirements and different filing agencies. We research what's needed for your specific event, file on your behalf, and follow up to make sure everything is confirmed before event day. It's one less thing for you to worry about.
Can you do ongoing work or retainers?
+
Yes. If you're a brand that runs seasonal activations, multiple events per year, or wants a consistent NYC presence across a campaign, we offer retainer and campaign-level pricing. This gives you priority scheduling, dedicated account management, and better rates across the board. Reach out and we'll put together a custom proposal based on your calendar and goals.
