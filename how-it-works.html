Phase 1 · Week 1

You reach out.
We respond within 24 hours.

The first step is simple: tell us what you're working toward. You don't need a finished brief, a locked budget, or a specific date. Even a rough idea is enough to get started. We'll ask the right questions, understand your goals, and put together a full written proposal within 48 hours of your inquiry — no commitment required on your end.

💬

Fill out our inquiry form

Share your brand name, event type, rough budget range, and timeline. The more context you give us, the more tailored our proposal will be — but even a few sentences is enough to get started.

📞

Discovery call (30–45 min)

We schedule a video call to go deeper: your brand story, your audience, your must-haves, your deal-breakers. This is where we start to understand whether we're the right fit for each other — and we'll be honest if we're not.

📄

Proposal in 48 hours

A clear written proposal covering our recommended approach, a rough budget breakdown, a sample timeline, and our production fee. No fluff, no vague language — just what you need to make a decision.

What to include in your first message

Don't overthink it. Here's what's useful to know up front:

  • Your brand name and what you sell or do
  • What kind of event you have in mind (pop-up, launch, dinner, activation)
  • A rough sense of your budget — even a range like "$10k–$30k" is helpful
  • Your preferred timing or target date
  • Any specific neighborhoods, venues, or aesthetics you have in mind
  • Where you're based (so we know how remote this needs to be)
What happens after you submit
  • ✓ You receive a confirmation within 2 hours during business hours
  • ✓ We review your inquiry and reach out to schedule a discovery call
  • ✓ Discovery call scheduled within 1–2 business days
  • ✓ Written proposal delivered within 48 hours of the call
  • ✓ Proposal includes: approach, budget range, timeline, and our fee
  • ✓ No commitment, no deposit, no obligation at this stage
  • ✓ You have as much time as you need to review and ask questions
2
Phase 2 · Week 2

We build the plan.
You approve every step.

Once you've signed the contract and paid the 50% deposit, the real work begins. We go deep on your brand, scout venues, develop the creative direction, and start sourcing vendors. Nothing moves forward without your explicit approval — every moodboard, every venue option, every vendor selection comes to you first.

🗺️

Venue scouting begins

We identify 3–5 venues across the right NYC neighborhoods for your brand. Each option comes with a video walkthrough, spec sheet, rental cost, availability, and our honest recommendation. You choose — or you tell us what's missing and we keep looking.

🎨

Moodboard & creative direction

We develop a visual direction for the space — layout, color palette, materials, signage, lighting references, and furniture style. This is presented as a moodboard for your review. If it's not right, we revise. Nothing gets built until the direction is locked.

🤝

Vendor sourcing starts

Based on your approved direction, we begin reaching out to our vendor network: fabrication, furniture rental, florals, A/V, signage, staffing. We get quotes, negotiate rates, and present options to you with a clear recommendation before committing to anything.

Your approvals during planning
  • ✓ Venue shortlist — you select from our top options
  • ✓ Creative moodboard — approved before any design work proceeds
  • ✓ Space layout — reviewed and confirmed before build begins
  • ✓ Vendor selections — you see who we're using and what they cost
  • ✓ Budget line-by-line — approved before any vendor deposits are paid
  • ✓ Staffing plan — headcount, roles, hours confirmed with you
  • ✓ Permit strategy — we explain what's needed and you sign off

How we handle decisions when you're remote

All approvals happen asynchronously through a shared Google Drive folder. Every venue option, moodboard revision, and vendor quote is uploaded there with our notes and recommendation. You review on your schedule, leave comments, and we action them — no time zone confusion, no dropped email threads.

  • Shared Drive folder set up at project kickoff
  • Every document labeled with version and date
  • Milestone check-in calls at each major approval stage
  • WhatsApp or Slack for quick questions between calls
3
Phase 3 · Weeks 3–4

Everything gets confirmed.
No loose ends.

This is where we shift from planning to confirming. The venue is booked, all vendors are under contract, permits are filed, and the run-of-show document starts to take shape. By the end of this phase, the event exists on paper in complete detail — every arrival time, every vendor role, every contingency.

📋

Permits filed

We file everything that's required with the relevant NYC agencies — whether that's sidewalk usage with the DOT, sound amplification permits, or temporary food service licensing. We follow up until everything is confirmed in writing, so there are no surprises on event day.

🔒

All vendors contracted

Every vendor is under a signed agreement with clear deliverables, arrival times, and cancellation terms. Deposits are paid from your event escrow — never out of our pocket. You receive a full vendor list with contact details for your records.

🗓️

Run-of-show drafted

A minute-by-minute schedule covering load-in, setup, event hours, and teardown. Every vendor arrival time, staff assignment, and logistical note is in here. It gets shared with you for review and then distributed to all involved parties 1 week before the event.

What "locked in" actually means

By the end of Phase 3, you should be able to step away entirely and know the event will happen exactly as planned. That means:

  • Venue contract signed, deposit paid, access confirmed
  • All permits confirmed in writing from NYC agencies
  • Every vendor under contract with locked pricing
  • Staffing headcount finalized and scheduled
  • Run-of-show v1 shared with all parties
  • Your remaining balance invoice issued (due 7 days before event)
The final balance trigger
  • ✓ Your remaining 50% production fee is due 7 days before event day
  • ✓ Final event budget reconciliation presented — you approve before any remaining vendor payments are made
  • ✓ Any unused float from your event budget is returned post-event
  • ✓ We never request more money than what was agreed in the proposal without your explicit approval
  • ✓ All international payments via Wise — no hidden currency fees
4
Phase 4 · Final week before

Staff briefed. Space built.
Everything in place.

The week before the event is when it all comes together physically. We brief every staff member on your brand, conduct venue walkthroughs with key vendors, confirm all delivery windows, and do a final check against the run-of-show. You'll hear from us at least once a day this week.

👥

Staff brand briefing

Every person working your event — whether that's a brand ambassador, a check-in host, or a sales associate — receives a written brand brief and attends a briefing call before the event. They know your product, your tone, your talking points, and your non-negotiables. They represent you as if they work for you.

🏗️

Load-in & build day

We're on-site from the first vendor arrival. We manage the build chronologically — furniture in first, then fixtures, then signage, then product placement and final styling. We send you photos throughout the day so you can see it coming together in real time. Final styling walkthrough is sent to you for approval before doors open.

✅

Final checks

The night before or morning of: every vendor has confirmed arrival. Run-of-show is distributed to all parties. Contingency plans are in place for weather, no-shows, and tech issues. You receive a "we're ready" confirmation with final photos before the event opens to the public.

What our staff briefing covers

We don't just hand staff a fact sheet. Our brand briefings are tailored to your specific event and include: brand overview and values, product knowledge and key selling points, how to greet guests and handle common questions, what's on-limits and off-limits to say, how to handle press or influencer requests, escalation protocol (who to call for any issue), and your post-event goals (email captures, sales targets, social tags). You approve the brief before it goes out.

5
Phase 5 · Event day(s)

We're on the ground.
You're in the loop.

This is what all the preparation is for. We're on-site from load-in to teardown, managing every moving part so the day runs exactly as planned. You're kept updated in real time — photos, attendance numbers, notes on what's working — so you feel present even from the other side of the world.

⏱️

Morning check-in

Every event day starts with a photo drop to you: the space set up and ready, staff in position, doors about to open. You know it looks right before a single guest walks in.

📲

Live updates throughout

Foot traffic numbers, notable guests, any adjustments we've made, candid photos — we send these throughout the day. You're not waiting until it's over to find out how it went.

🌙

End-of-day wrap

Each evening we send a day-of summary: estimated attendance, content captured, any press or influencer visits, what worked well, and anything we're adjusting for tomorrow if it's a multi-day event.

What we manage on the day so you don't have to

  • Vendor arrivals, deliveries, and load-in sequence
  • Staff clock-in, positioning, and performance
  • Guest flow, queuing, and capacity management
  • Photographer and content creator coordination
  • Press and influencer check-in and hospitality
  • Real-time problem solving — power issues, late vendors, weather, anything
  • Teardown coordination and venue handover
If something goes wrong
  • ✓ We carry general liability insurance on all events
  • ✓ Every budget includes a 5–10% contingency float for day-of issues
  • ✓ We have backup vendors on call for last-minute cancellations
  • ✓ You'll be notified immediately if anything significant changes
  • ✓ We handle the fix — you don't need to make calls or chase anyone
  • ✓ Post-event debrief will cover what happened and how it was resolved
6
Phase 6 · Within 5 business days

Teardown done. Report delivered.
Results in your hands.

The event is over — but we're not done yet. Within 5 business days of your final event day, you receive a comprehensive post-event report covering everything your team needs to understand the ROI, present results internally, and plan the next one smarter.

📊

Post-event analytics report

Estimated total attendance broken down by day, foot traffic patterns, busiest windows, and comparison to projections. If you had sales targets, we include sell-through data. If you had a sign-up goal, we include capture rates.

📸

Content assets delivered

All photography and video organized in a shared Drive folder — hero shots, lifestyle images, behind-the-scenes, and video clips. Everything is labeled and ready for your team to use. We include a recommended usage note: which assets are best for which channels.

💬

Honest debrief

What worked exactly as planned. What we'd do differently next time. Vendor performance notes. Guest feedback if collected. Our recommendations for your next NYC activation, including timing, budget, and format. This is the section most clients say is the most valuable.

What's in the full post-event report

  • Total attendance + per-day breakdown
  • Estimated social reach from content and tags
  • Press coverage secured (articles, mentions, features)
  • Influencer content delivered and estimated reach
  • Final budget reconciliation — every dollar accounted for
  • Unused float returned with itemized statement
  • Vendor performance ratings (useful for future events)
  • Our full debrief and recommendations for next time
Budget reconciliation explained
  • ✓ Full line-by-line statement of every vendor payment made
  • ✓ Any unused event budget float is returned to you within 5 business days
  • ✓ If any unplanned cost was approved by you mid-event, it's documented here with the approval timestamp
  • ✓ No hidden "admin fees" or post-event additions
  • ✓ International clients receive reconciliation in USD with Wise transfer confirmation

Real-time photo updates on event days

Money & payments

How the money works — clearly.

One of the most common concerns new clients have is getting into a situation where they're out of pocket or surprised by a bill. We've built our payment structure specifically to prevent that from happening — for both sides.

1
50% production fee at contract signing

This kicks off the project and secures your spot in our calendar. It covers our planning time, venue scouting, moodboard development, and all work done before vendors are engaged. Due via invoice at the time you sign — wire transfer, card, or Wise for international clients.

2
Event budget funded before vendor payments

Before we pay any vendor, you fund the event escrow with the agreed event budget. We pay vendors from this account with your sign-off. You receive a statement of every payment made. We never front vendor costs from our own pocket.

3
Remaining 50% due 7 days before event

The final balance of our production fee is due one week before your event. At this point, all vendors are confirmed and the run-of-show is locked. After the event, any unused budget float is returned to you within 5 business days with a full reconciliation statement.

Questions

Things people always
want to know

What if I don't have a clear brief yet?
+
That's completely fine — and honestly, most of our best projects started with a vague idea. Fill out the inquiry form with whatever you know: your brand, a rough budget range, and something about what you want to achieve. We'll guide you through the rest on the discovery call. Our job is to ask the right questions and help you figure out what you actually need.
How quickly can you turn around a pop-up?
+
Our standard lead time is 4–6 weeks for a well-produced activation. In some cases we've moved faster — 2–3 weeks for simpler activations with flexible venue requirements. If your timeline is tight, tell us upfront. We'll be straight with you about what's realistic rather than overpromise and underdeliver.
Can I make changes mid-project?
+
Yes — up to a point. Minor changes (a different table layout, swapping a floral color, adjusting staff headcount) can usually be accommodated without issue. Significant changes — new venue, major aesthetic shift, event date change — may affect cost and timeline. We'll always tell you the impact before actioning anything, and nothing changes without your written approval.
What happens if I need to cancel?
+
Cancellation terms are outlined clearly in your contract. Generally: the 50% deposit is non-refundable once planning has begun, as it covers real work already done. Any event budget that hasn't been committed to vendors is returned to you in full. Vendor deposits already paid are subject to each vendor's own cancellation policy — we'll always flag these before you commit to anything. We handle postponements differently from cancellations — reach out and we'll work with you.
Do I need to attend the event?
+
No — and many of our clients don't. We're built for remote clients. You'll receive live photo updates, end-of-day summaries, and a full debrief after. If you want to attend, that's always welcome. If you want to join via video call during a key moment, we can arrange that too. But your physical presence is never required for the event to run perfectly.
How do you handle unexpected costs on the day?
+
Every budget includes a 5–10% contingency float for exactly this reason. Small day-of costs (extra supplies, a quick fix) come from this float without interrupting you. For anything larger or out of scope, we call you immediately, explain what's needed and why, and wait for your approval before spending. We never commit your money without your say-so.
What's the minimum budget needed to work with you?
+
Our Essentials package starts at a $3,500 production fee, and we typically work with event budgets starting around $8,000–10,000. Below that, it becomes difficult to produce something in NYC that reflects well on your brand — venue costs, permits, and basic vendor minimums add up quickly in this city. We'd rather tell you that upfront than take your money for something that won't deliver the result you want.
Can you handle multiple events or an ongoing campaign?
+
Yes — and we love this kind of work. If you're planning a seasonal calendar, a multi-event campaign, or want an ongoing NYC presence, we offer retainer and campaign pricing that gives you priority scheduling, a dedicated account manager, and better vendor rates across the board. Reach out and we'll put together a custom proposal based on your calendar.
